# Scenario: museum loan dispatch M-12

Synthetic and exposed development material, not held-out evaluation. A conservation-readiness certificate is not dispatch approval. A registrar must separately approve departure. An emergency move within the building may precede approval only with two handlers, continuous location tracking and same-day registrar notification. It does not authorize an external loan. The conservation pilot is limited to training replicas; treatment of an undocumented adhesive is unknown.

The fixture interpreter deliberately omits same-day notification. Challenger raises that consequential omission. The operator can correct it, reject it or explicitly accept the challenged version. A later intent revision creates a new proposal. A recipient then gets a novel dispatch question withheld from the sender. It should distinguish readiness from approval and preserve the emergency exception conditions. Correct field repetition alone is not demonstrated understanding.

Five-minute sequence: create exchange, interpret, challenge, inspect source anchor, enter correction, accept current version, preview each packet, deliver to fresh recipient, compare, export. Fixtures illustrate the mechanism only. Human decisions made by automated tests are marked TEST_OPERATOR, not genuine human acceptance.
